{%- comment -%} One secondary role's name list in the Chicago voice — the editors, translators or interviewers that sit below the author block in publication-cite.html. Splits the raw " and "-joined front-matter field and joins it through credit-join.html, taking the serial comma from three names up (Chicago's rule), so a list of two joins on a bare " and ". Emits nothing when the field is absent. The Dario-stripping counterpart, for the homepage's short voice, is credit-names.html. Params: include.raw — a front-matter name field (item.editor, item.translator, …) {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.raw -%}{%- assign _r = include.raw | split: ' and ' -%}{%- assign _sc = false -%}{%- if _r.size > 2 -%}{%- assign _sc = true -%}{%- endif -%}{%- include credit-join.html items=_r serial_comma=_sc -%}{%- endif -%}
