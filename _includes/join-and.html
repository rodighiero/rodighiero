{%- comment -%} Joins an array of strings with ", " and "and" before the last item — shared by name-list.html (homepage byline) and citation.html (Chicago reference). Each item is stripped before joining. Params: include.items - array of strings to join include.serial_comma - truthy for ", and X" (Chicago style); falsy for " and X" (no Oxford comma) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _out = "" -%} {%- for _n in include.items -%} {%- assign _n = _n | strip -%} {%- if forloop.first -%}{%- assign _out = _n -%} {%- elsif forloop.last -%} {%- if include.serial_comma -%}{%- assign _out = _out | append: ", and " | append: _n -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign _out = _out | append: " and " | append: _n -%}{%- endif -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign _out = _out | append: ", " | append: _n -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- _out -}}
