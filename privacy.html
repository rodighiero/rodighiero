Alfred Drive Search — Privacy

Alfred Drive Search is a personal tool that lets its owner search his own Google Drive from Alfred on macOS. It is not offered to the public.

It asks Google for read-only access to file names and metadata. It cannot open, change or delete files. Search results are shown only in Alfred on the user’s own computer, the sign-in token is kept in the macOS Keychain, and nothing is collected, stored elsewhere or shared with anyone.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account settings, or by typing drive:logout in Alfred.
