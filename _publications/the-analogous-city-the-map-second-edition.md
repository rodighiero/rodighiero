Few exhibition panels have had as much impact as _La città analoga_ (The Analogous City), which Aldo Rossi presented with Eraldo Consolascio, Bruno Reichlin and Fabio Reinhart at the 1976 Venice Biennale: a vision for urban design that takes a creative approach to the historical richness of architecture, and that seeks a connection between reality and imagination as an alternative to a narrow, functional way of thinking. This second, revised edition of the map — first published by EPFL Archizoom in 2015 — accompanies _Darling Architecture_, the permanent exhibition with which the Bonnefantenmuseum marks the thirtieth anniversary of the building Rossi designed for it. Printed as a foldable sheet and published open access, it reproduces the original work, locates on a lettered grid the references identified within the collage, and carries texts by Aldo Rossi, Fabio Reinhart and Dario Rodighiero in English and Dutch.

## Another Form of the City to Visit

_Dario Rodighiero_

In 1976, Aldo Rossi and his friends presented a large panel they called _La città analoga_ (The Analogous City) — a collage of projects, buildings, and references proposing an alternative way to look at the city (Rossi 1976). This map is an interpretation of that panel. As Fabio Reinhart (2015) writes, to live on is to be transformed yet remain the same. So the collage becomes a foldable map, another form of the city to visit. Each fragment carries a reference drawn from books, libraries, and archives, sedimented over time into conversations. A work like this is never static; it is made present again through the choices of those who archive, curate, and read it (Rodighiero et al. 2026). The first edition of the map (2015) marked the fortieth anniversary of the Analogous City; the second, in your hands, celebrates the thirtieth of the Bonnefanten, the museum Rossi himself designed. Today, this map asks for no instructions — take it, wander, and let the city mean what you find in it.

## This Edition

The sheet is printed on both sides. The recto reproduces the panel at large format, its margins lettered A to O and numbered 1 to 15 so that every fragment can be named by its coordinates. The verso gathers Aldo Rossi’s text of 1976, Fabio Reinhart’s captions of 2015 and the catalog of references, each entry giving the coordinates of the fragment, its author, place and date, and the book the image was taken from — the archaeology that returns the collage to its sources.

This edition is bilingual in English and Dutch: Christopher Stead translated Fabio Reinhart’s text, and Ton Quik translated Reinhart’s, Rossi’s and Rodighiero’s. Research support came from Fabio Reinhart, Beatrice Lampariello, Ton Quik, Luca Ortelli, Nicola Braghieri, Sabina Rosenbergová and Erica Bernardi. The map is published open access by University of Groningen Press and is free to take at the Bonnefantenmuseum, where the panel hangs in _Darling Architecture_, the museum’s permanent tribute to its architect.
