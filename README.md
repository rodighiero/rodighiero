Knowledge design is Dario Rodighiero’s mode of inquiry — a way of doing research that borrows its questions from digital humanities, its visual grammar from information design, and its public address from science communication. Two strands organize the work: one focuses on research communities, as in [*Mapping Affinities*](/mapping-affinities-democratizing-data-visualization) and [*Grounding AI Map*](/grounding-ai-map-the-consequences-of-living-with-the-trouble-of-an-irreductionist-map), and one turns to cultural heritage, as in [*Surprise Machines*](/surprise-machines-revealing-harvard-art-museums-image-collection) and the [*Map of the Analogous City*](/the-analogous-city-the-map). Across both strands, digital archives are transformed into a form others can read, question, and reuse.

At the [University of Groningen](https://www.rug.nl/), he is Assistant Professor of Science and Technology Studies at [Campus Fryslân](https://www.rug.nl/cf/), the university’s interdisciplinary faculty. There he coordinates the minor [Data Wise](https://www.rug.nl/cf/education/minors/data-wise/) and teaches data and visual literacy in the bachelor’s program [Data Science and Society](https://www.rug.nl/cf/education/bachelor/bsc-data-science-and-society/). He is a principal at [metaLAB](https://metalabharvard.github.io/) (at) Harvard / Berlin / Basel, a center engaged in critical and creative practice across the disciplinary grid. He is co-chair of the [IEEE VIS Arts Program](https://visap.net/) for the 2025 and 2026 editions in Vienna and Boston.

Dario is an alumnus of [MIT](https://www.mit.edu/), [Harvard](https://www.harvard.edu/), and [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/). At EPFL he attended the doctoral program in Architecture and Sciences of the City. He held research positions at the Berkman Klein Center, the [Joint Research Centre](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/), and [Sciences Po](https://www.sciencespo.fr/), taught at [Paris-Sorbonne University](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/) and the [Free University of Bozen-Bolzano](https://www.unibz.it/en/), and was visiting professor at [FU Berlin](https://www.fu-berlin.de/) and [Paris Nanterre University](https://www.parisnanterre.fr/). Dario has lectured at venues such as [CERN](https://home.cern/) and [Ars Electronica](https://ars.electronica.art/) and exhibited his work at institutions including [MAXXI](https://www.maxxi.art/) in Rome and the [Harvard Art Museums](https://harvardartmuseums.org/).
